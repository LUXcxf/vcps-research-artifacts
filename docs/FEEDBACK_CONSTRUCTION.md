# Candidate Feedback Construction

VCPS constructs comparison supervision from alternative legal skills at the same observable training state. The released comparisons support residual training without an additional expert ranking of these alternatives.

## ALFWorld Collection Protocol

1. Use source observations and skill histories from the scale-specific training task pool. Restore each source state by resetting its game and replaying the recorded history; check the restored observation against the source observation.
2. Select alternatives from the environment's admissible commands. The D240 collector excludes help, inventory and look, covers available action schemas, and fills the remaining slots with a stable hash ordering, up to 16 alternatives per state. Candidate selection does not consult an expert next-skill label.
3. Execute the candidate first. Continue with a fixed observable-context heuristic: filter legal commands for goal consistency and cycles, rank them by the progress prior, and execute the first-ranked command. This is a collection policy, not the trained residual or an online update of the evaluated actor.
4. A branch contains at most eight executed skills, including the initial candidate, and can end earlier when the environment terminates or no continuation is available. The restored environment has a 30-step total episode limit, including the replayed history. Branch success is the environment's goal-completion flag.
5. Form preferences only between successful and unsuccessful branches at the same source state. If a state has no successful branch or no unsuccessful branch, it contributes no comparison. Among available branches, retain up to four successful and eight unsuccessful alternatives, ordered by the recorded trace return and then the skill string. Cross their retained positive and negative sets and assign each pair weight `1 / (positive_count * negative_count)`.
6. Split comparisons by source-task identity using the deterministic 80/20 SHA-1 fold. Training and holdout comparisons never share a source task. Compile the residual inputs from the observable source context and candidate skills.

The resulting preference describes goal completion under the fixed continuation policy and branch budget. It is candidate-level progress supervision; it is not a measurement of the globally optimal remaining distance. The heuristic continuation and trace-return ordering are part of this supervision-construction protocol, rather than extra runtime components added to the evaluated VCPS policy.

## D240 Record Correspondence

The retained collection contains 21,578 branch records from 1,403 source states, including 7,813 successful branches. Applying the selection, weighting and task split above reconstructs the candidate identities, ordering and weights of all 7,309 released training comparisons and 1,116 holdout comparisons.

The audited raw branch record has SHA-256 `c432873cccd8c4697c42537a897b452aaaa3b063e8bd720c1efb6a0faa52cbc2`. The public training inputs are `data/feedback/d240_train.jsonl` and `data/feedback/d240_holdout.jsonl`; their identities are bound by `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`.

The release includes the original branch records, losslessly compressed as `data/feedback/candidates_d240.jsonl.gz`, the 1,403 observable source states in `data/feedback/source_states_d240.jsonl`, and their 240-task training manifest. Source-state records retain the observed histories and admissible skills needed for replay; they contain no expert next-skill label or expert candidate rank.

`scripts/collect_feedback_candidates.py` performs state replay and bounded candidate execution through the released environment bridge. Its collection-only continuation policy is supplied in `src/feedback_collection_policy.py`; predicate and return construction are in `src/feedback_collection_outcomes.py`. Defaults are 16 candidates, an eight-skill branch, a 30-step restored episode and discount 0.9. New collection writes separate records and an input-identity sidecar; `--resume` checks source, implementation and settings identities before continuing.

`scripts/build_candidate_pairs.py` reconstructs comparisons directly from branch outcomes, including selection, weighting, task split and all observable features. `scripts/verify_feedback_construction.py` checks equality of all 8,425 reconstructed comparison records against the released training and holdout files. `scripts/build_residual_pairs.py` remains the feature-reconstruction entry point for existing comparisons. Neither operation needs a GPU; new environment collection uses the pinned ALFWorld bridge environment.

## Materials Workflow Instance

The companion constructs candidate successor states through its workflow verifier and obtains continuation lengths from its contract planner. These lengths induce same-state comparisons for the feedback residual. The domain actor demonstrations are generated by the same planner on the training split. The companion supplies the contract, planner, training-view construction and matched evaluation records under `workstation/`.
